Short reminders of ideas the lessons use but do not teach from scratch. Each section is referenced from the lesson that first needs it.

## Asymptotic notation

For nonnegative functions $f$ and $g$, we write $f(n) \in \mathcal{O}(g(n))$ if there are constants $C$ and $n_0$ with $f(n) \le C g(n)$ for all $n \ge n_0$. The definition suppresses constants so that growth rates can be compared; it does not say constants never matter. First used in Lesson 3.

## Counting

Products, sums, and binomial coefficients as they arise in counting alignments, paths in a grid, and trees. First used in Lesson 3.

## Probability

Random variables, conditional probability, independence, and the difference between the probability of data under a model and the plausibility of a model given data. First used in Lesson 9.

## Linear algebra

Orthonormal bases, projections, and the singular value decomposition. First used in Lesson 16.

## Differential equations

Fixed points of $\dot{x} = f(x)$ and their stability from the sign of $f'$. First used in Lesson 21.
