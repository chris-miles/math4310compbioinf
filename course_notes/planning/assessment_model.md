# Assessment Model

The approved schedule contains ten problem sets and two in-class midterms. Midterm 1 is February 24 and covers Lessons 1–7; Midterm 2 is March 31 and covers Lessons 8–14. Each follows the last homework deadline for its covered material by one week. See [the lesson calendar](lesson_calendar.md).

Assessment weights, project format, and any final assessment remain undecided. April 26 is Flex; no project presentation meetings or finals-week project deadline are reserved. Do not treat earlier project-weight proposals as policy.

Exams should assess individual reasoning through small recurrences, alignment tables and traceback, assembly graphs, sequence likelihoods, HMM calculations, and tree inference. Avoid long code, memorized tool names, or large biological vocabulary. PCA, single-feature comparisons, FDR, clustering, and dynamics are covered by later problem sets and class practice.

Any future project decision should specify its purpose, workload, grading, collaboration format, and dates before student-facing requirements are added. A bounded dataset, one course method, an independent validation case, and an explanation of limitations are useful design criteria if that option is adopted.
