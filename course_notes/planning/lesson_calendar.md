This leaves **27 meetings: 24 lessons, two midterms, and one combined project workshop/work session**. There is no extra full-class project work day.

## Assessment timing

Midterm 1 follows Lesson 8 and covers Lessons 1 to 8. Midterm 2 follows spring break and covers Lessons 9 to 13. Phylogenetics begins after Midterm 2. Each lesson occupies its own meeting; the HMM inference classes are not compressed to make up for holidays.

Ten problem sets are released after the relevant Wednesday lesson (or the last lesson listed that week). Canvas is the submission authority. The first set is designed for about one hour. Later drafts should keep coding compact and concentrate on interpretable evidence.
