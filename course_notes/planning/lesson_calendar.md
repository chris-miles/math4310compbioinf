## Assessment timing

Midterm 1 is February 24 and covers Lessons 1 to 8; the final homework for that material is due February 17. Midterm 2 is March 31 and covers Lessons 9 to 15: probabilistic sequence models and phylogenetics; the phylogenetics homework is due March 24. Each exam comes one week after the last relevant homework deadline. New topics begin during the study interval; PCA and multiple testing are outside Midterm 2 coverage.

Ten problem sets are released after the relevant Wednesday lesson (or the last lesson listed that week). Canvas is the submission authority. The first set is designed for about one hour. Later drafts should keep coding compact and concentrate on interpretable evidence.

April 21 and April 26 are reserved for project presentations. With about 25 students working mainly in pairs, allow for 12 or 13 groups. Eight minutes presenting plus two minutes of questions per group takes 120 to 130 minutes, distributed across the two meetings. Final repository submission remains in finals week; no exact deadline is invented here.
