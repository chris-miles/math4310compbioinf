## HB261 / public-posting notes

- For mandatory courses, current USHE guidance requires public searchable posting before the course starts. The U routes this through Simple Syllabus and CIS.
- MATH 4310 is "mandatory" only if a degree program requires this exact course for graduation and no alternative course can satisfy the same requirement. Confirm against the degree audit or department list before posting.
- The syllabus should not include internal legal rationale. It only needs to satisfy the content requirements above.
- The current durable-skills language avoids mandatory identity-based submissions and keeps cross-disciplinary audience work under Communication.
